scf::property_types::DECLARE_PROPERTY_TYPE ( ConvergenceUncertainty  )

Attaches the uncertainty from incomplete convergence to an energy.

Called by an iterative method once it has converged. The result is the energy with uncertainty attached, e.g., from the change in the energy in the final iteration.

scf::property_types::DECLARE_PROPERTY_TYPE ( MOUncertainty  )

Attaches uncertainty to converged molecular orbitals.

Called by an SCF once it has converged. The orbital energies and coefficients are the eigenpairs of the converged Fock matrix in the metric of the overlap matrix. The result is the coefficients with uncertainty attached, e.g., from the uncertainty in the Fock matrix and from incomplete convergence.
